Game Title: Ror-ro

This was my first attempt at creating a 2D game, including all the code and graphical abilities. 

The story follows a young, small blue creature by the name or "Ror-ro". Born and raised amidst a jungle tribe, Ror-ro lived a rigorous and tough life. He believed that, simply, this was how things were to be. He didn’t struggle amongst struggle, he lived with life.

There was a story passed around throughout generations of tribesmen, a blight of darkness brought about by a single being of pure darkness. It was said that this being was cold, ruthless, angry… all the adjectives for a malevolent being incarnate, that would appear hidden, dormant - and pop with a terrifying horror unknown, bringing death and discolouration to the world around them. 

Without knowing, the player only being able to put two and two together at the end, that being? Was Ror-ro. 

“You’ve been wishing to leave everything for a while now. This is your chance.”
