Professionally, I’m a writer and editor, and aspiring Composer, Game dev and artist. 

But personally, I’m… well, the same, but I’ve also got character! Yeah!

My name is Corey Canning, and I’ve had stakes in the gaming industry ever since I was young. The first job I wanted was to be a vet, so I could help animals! Only to find out I’d have to kill some to do so, and changed my mind. I think at like 7? At the time, the other option was to be a Game Dev! So I studied, got my A-levels and started University for that exact course!

But beforehand, I’d already tried my hand here-and-there with minor projects, like working with RPG Maker and pixel art, but the majority of my time has been spent honing the craft of writing~

Consuming many different media’s, from novels to anime to movies, I’ve found the best medium for story telling- is actually video games. Whether you’re a blank mc that you can self-insert with, or an established character like Kratos from the GOW series, the fact that you get to PLAY as the character, makes you all the more invested in a story. In horror, you feel scared, as you’re in control of the character scared for their life, etc. Now, don’t get me wrong, many of my inspirations like FF7, ISAT and Chrono Trigger have stellar stories, but video game writing really isn’t given the praise it deserves, and most people believe you play a game to shut off, or to relax - undermining the desire for incredible story telling.
But the interactivity of gaming, the variables and switches to change the outcomes, the music and graphics, some interesting and dynamic mechanics - you combine all that into a high-tier written novel, and you’d have a masterpiece, no?

Whilst I’m working on the prior skills, I think leaning into the latter is essential to where my worth lies as a game developer. 

So, you have an idea for a game? Need plot points, diverse characters, emotional moments to connect players, mechanics rooted within the world, a complex morality carried by the mc? 
It’d be an honour to work with you, as I enjoy creating all those things~
(And, there’s a high chance I already have…)
